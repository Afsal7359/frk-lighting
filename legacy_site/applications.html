Applications

Solar lighting suits almost any place that needs light and has sky above it.

Roads and highways

Panchayat roads, bypasses, junctions and bridges. Split-type lights give even light over long runs with no cabling.

Residential layouts and apartments

Internal roads, gates and compound walls. Quiet, no bills for the residents' association.

Car parks

Malls, hospitals, offices, churches and temples. Wide, even light makes parking safer at night.

Schools, colleges and campuses

Pathways, play areas and gates, without trenching across lawns or playgrounds.

Factories, warehouses and yards

Perimeter security and loading areas. Flood lights up to 600 W cover large spaces.

Farms, plantations and remote sites

Places where a power line would cost more than the lights. Solar is the practical choice.

Resorts, parks and tourist spots

Garden and pathway lights add atmosphere without visible cables.

Construction and temporary sites

Lights can be moved when the project moves on.
