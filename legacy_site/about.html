About FRK Lighting

A Kerala supplier of solar street lights, built on clear advice and honest sizing.

Who we are

FRK Lighting Solutions supplies solar street lights, flood lights and pathway lights for businesses, residential projects and public spaces across Kerala.

Too many buyers get a spec sheet and no advice. We do it differently. We ask about the road, the shade and the weather, then recommend a light that fits. If a smaller system will do, we say so.

We handle the full job: choosing the right product, supplying poles and fittings, installing on site and supporting you afterwards.

How we work

Honest sizing

We size the panel and battery for the rainy season, not the sunniest week, so the lights do not fade by midnight.

Clear quotations

Lights, poles, foundations, transport and installation are listed line by line.

Local support

We are in Kerala. If a light has a problem, we can reach you and speak your language.

Complete service

Survey, supply, civil work, installation and after-sales from one team.

What happens on a project

  1. Site details

    Send photos, a sketch or a location and the number of lights.

  2. Recommendation and quote

    We propose a system with a written quote.

  3. Supply and install

    Foundations, poles, lights and testing on site.

  4. Handover and support

    You get warranty terms and a number to call.
