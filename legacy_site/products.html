Products

Solar street lights, flood lights and pathway lights. Final wattage, pole height and battery type are confirmed in your quotation.

All-in-one solar street light

The solar panel, battery, controller and LED sit in one housing. Mount it on a pole, aim the panel at the sun and it is ready.

  • Best for: estate roads, lanes, paths, gates and small compounds
  • Why choose it: fastest installation and a tidy look
  • Keep in mind: panel size is fixed to the body, so very long hours may need the split type

Split-type solar street light

The panel is mounted separately on top of the pole, with a battery box on the pole or in the ground. You can size the panel and battery to the job.

  • Best for: main roads, car parks, long hours and cloudy locations
  • Why choose it: larger capacity, and the battery can be replaced on its own
  • Keep in mind: slightly more installation work than all-in-one

Solar flood light

A wide, bright beam for areas rather than roads. Models are available up to 600 W for large grounds.

  • Best for: yards, sports grounds, parking, building perimeters and signage
  • Why choose it: strong output with no wiring to the building
  • Keep in mind: mount at the right height and angle to avoid glare

Garden and pathway lights

Low-height solar lights for walkways, lawns, resort paths and entrances, where a tall pole would be too much.

  • Best for: gardens, resorts, parks and compound walls
  • Why choose it: soft light, simple to place anywhere
